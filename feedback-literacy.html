What Do You Do With Feedback?

Before you start

This week is about feedback, the giving and the getting. Take a few minutes to look at what you actually do with feedback at work, and complete this snapshot.

Pick one job, role, or context and keep it in mind for all 24 statements. Your current job works best. If you aren't working right now, use a past job or a role you've put real effort into, like an internship, a volunteer role, or a team. Wherever a statement says "work," read it as the work you do in that role.

There's no score to aim for. This snapshot earns completion credit, and I'm interested in what you notice. Your answers stay in this browser window and aren't sent to me or saved anywhere, so be sure to take a screenshot of your name, role, and results before you exit out of this window.
