How Do You Behave in a Group?

Before you read

This week is about what leaders do: how much of their attention goes to the task, and how much goes to the people. Before you read what researchers found, take about five minutes to notice what you already do.

Pick one group you're part of often, like a work team, a study group, a club, or a volunteer crew. Keep that one group in mind for all 12 questions, and answer for how you usually act there. How you would like to act comes later in the course.

There's no score to aim for. This snapshot earns completion credit, and I'm interested in what you notice. Your answers stay in this browser window and aren't sent to me or saved anywhere, so take your screenshot before you close the page.
