Which Leadership Ideas Sound Like You?

Before you start

This week finishes our look at leadership theories. Before you are introduced the last three, take about ten minutes to see which ideas about leadership already resonate the most with you.

You'll see 15 pairs of beliefs about leadership. For each pair, split 5 points between the two statements, based on how much you agree with each. If you agree with both, that's fine. Give more points to the one you believe a little more. Five points can't split evenly, so you'll always lean one way, even if only slightly.

Each statement connects to one of six leadership theories: three from the last module and three from this one. Leadership theories are called that because there are no "right" answers to how to effectively lead others.

There's no score to aim for, just like the previous snapshots. I'm most interested in what you notice. I wrote these statements for this class; they're a reflection tool, not a published/research-based scale. Your answers stay in this browser window and aren't sent to me or saved anywhere, so be sure to take a screenshot of your name and results before you exit out of this window.
